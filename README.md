# Cargo Fridge Website

A modern, responsive business website for **Cargo Fridge**, a South African company specializing in transport refrigeration, cold rooms, freezer rooms, insulated load bodies, and custom refrigerated transport solutions.

---

## Overview

Cargo Fridge provides professional refrigeration solutions for the transport, logistics, agricultural, hunting, and cold-chain industries.

This website serves as the company's online presence and marketing platform, allowing potential customers to:

- Learn about the company and its services
- View completed projects
- Request quotations
- Contact the company directly
- Access emergency breakdown support information

---

## Target Audience

This website is designed for:

### Logistics & Transport Companies
Businesses requiring reliable refrigerated transport solutions.

### Food Industry
Companies involved in cold-chain distribution and food storage.

### Farmers & Agricultural Businesses
Producers requiring cold room and freezer room installations.

### Hunting & Game Farms
Customers needing insulated and refrigerated hunting trailers.

### Commercial & Industrial Clients
Organizations requiring custom refrigeration systems and maintenance services.
